Leaf charging dialogs · revamp

Recorded simulator output using the real firmware renderer. Full screens on the left are native size; the dialog on the right is enlarged at an exact integer scale. Both are aligned to physical display pixels, accounting for zoom and display density.

Leaf Log: 96 × 51 at y=141. USB/power: 96 × 23 at y=150, extended upward one pixel for equal text margins. Leaf Log interaction artwork and counts are raised one pixel. Paused animation cycles through zero, one, two, three dots. A missing card replaces the version with an SD icon and a 6 × 12 NO SD CARD! warning. Two taps confirm cancellation within five seconds; uploads continue during the question. Paused animation stays visible while USB is hidden. USB availability stays visible while the drive is exposed. Each session handles at most 100 eligible flights.
