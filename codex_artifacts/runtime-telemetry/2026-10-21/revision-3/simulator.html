Review notes and measurement limits
  • Normal slot/page statistics exclude held buttons, button events and recovery. Recovery covers the next two dispatched slots and extends while completion is late or start delay exceeds 1 ms; inspect it separately.
  • Host CPU work is uncalibrated. Bars are not ESP32 utilization; deadline crossings use virtual time.
  • Device heap and stack measurements are unavailable in the simulator.
  • GPS summaries include all sentence dispatches, including interactions. Stages are exclusive elapsed times on device (host thread CPU in sim), not isolated CPU costs. UART timestamps observe firmware reads, not wire arrival. Checksum acceptance does not establish a valid position fix.
  • Recent trace wrapped by design; full-run slot aggregates and the worst incident remain available.
  • Clock-pair, slot-bookkeeping, and memory-sampling costs are reported. Full instrumentation overhead requires an external A/B measurement.
  • Stage 1 manual capture: enabled features are not verified workload coverage. Review is required before release.
