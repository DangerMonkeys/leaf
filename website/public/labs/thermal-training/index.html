Tutorial

Once we enter a thermal and the vario peaks, should we turn left or right?

If we enter a thermal near the middle, it doesn't really matter. Either way, we'll end up off to one side and will need to adjust and center up. But if we enter near the edge, it becomes much more important: the correct way will take us around the center; the wrong way will dump us out the side.

There are two main signals to know which way to turn.

1. Sideways pull or shift

The rising thermal is also pulling in air from all sides. Further out from the thermal (anywhere from about 3 to 10 seconds before we hear the vario beep), we may be able to detect a slight sideways pull or shift toward the thermal. This can be quite subtle, and will be a sideways-slipping feeling, not a bank or turn. It's a good idea to turn toward the direction we're being pulled. (In the example diagram, we should turn right.)

2. Bank or roll just as we enter the thermal and the vario starts to beep

If we're off-center, the wingtip toward the center will be raised more and/or sooner, and we'll experience a bank away from the thermal. We should turn back the other way, toward the lifted wing. (In the example diagram, we should turn right.)

In this exercise, you will be presented with several invisible thermal entries. Fly straight until the vario peaks, then choose the best way to turn. Once you turn, the thermal will be shown.
