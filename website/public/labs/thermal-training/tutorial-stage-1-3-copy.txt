THERMAL TRAINING — IMPLEMENTED TUTORIAL COPY

This document records all tutorial wording and per-step presentation rules currently
implemented in the prototype. The tutorial currently contains two stages:

1. Center a Thermal — five steps
2. Thermal Sizes — three steps


STANDARD ACTIONS
======================================================================

Desktop before each step:
Press Space to start.

Desktop after each completed step:
Press Space to continue.

Desktop after the final step:
Press Space to finish.

STAGE 1 — CENTER A THERMAL
======================================================================

Stage structure:
Visual guidance is progressively removed across five steps.


STEP 1 — FIND THE HALFWAY POINT
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Presentation:
- Fuzzy green thermal, outer boundary, and concentric rings shown
- Green/red optimum turn-rate guidance hidden
- Flight track fully visible

Title:
Step 1: Find the "halfway point" into the thermal

Instructions:
Fly straight into the lift. Don't turn. When the vario peaks and then starts to decrease, you are halfway through the core. That is the moment to start turning.

Goal:
Fly straight, wait for the vario to peak, then turn about 90 degrees.

Popup guidance:
[If the user turns before the vario peak]
DON'T TURN
Fly straight until the vario peaks

[Immediately after the vario peaks]
START TURNING NOW
Turn about 90 degrees

Completion feedback:
Great! You found the halfway point and started the turn soon after the vario peak.


STEP 2 — TURN AROUND THE CENTER
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Presentation:
- Fuzzy green thermal, outer boundary, and concentric rings shown
- Green/red optimum turn-rate guidance hidden
- Flight track fully visible

Title:
Step 2: Turn around the center of the thermal

Instructions:
Now the goal is to keep the vario constant. If it starts to drop, you are moving away from the center and need to tighten your turn momentarily. If it starts to increase, you are moving toward the center and need to widen your turn momentarily

Goal:
Fly two consecutive orbits around the thermal. Don't get too close to the center or the edge.

Popup guidance:
[Constant vario]
VARIO STEADY
Hold a constant turn rate

[Decreasing vario]
VARIO DECREASING
tighten your turn to move closer to the center

[Increasing vario]
VARIO INCREASING
widen your turn to move away from the center

Completion feedback:
Great! You flew two centered orbits without drifting too close to the middle or the edge.


STEP 3 — OPTIMIZE YOUR TURN RATE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Presentation:
- Fuzzy green thermal, outer boundary, and concentric rings shown
- Green/red optimum turn-rate guidance shown
- Flight track fully visible

Title:
Step 3: Optimize your turn rate

Instructions:
The turn indicator will be green at the optimum bank angle.  Turning too steep will increase your descent rate, so don't stay in the red zone for long. Center your circles around the thermal and stay as close to the optimal turn rate as you can.

Goal:
Fly 2 consecutive orbits staying near optimal turn-rate while staying centered on the thermal.

Completion feedback:
Great! You stayed centered while keeping the turn rate near the efficient range.

Transition feedback:
Great job, you centered the thermal at the optimum turn rate. Next, practice again without the visual circle help. The fuzzy green thermal is still shown, but rely on the vario sound for most of your guidance.


STEP 4 — PUT IT ALL TOGETHER
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Flow:
After the user starts, the simulation runs continuously through finding the vario
peak, turning about 90 degrees, centering, and completing two optimized orbits.
There is no intermediate pause.

Presentation:
- Fuzzy green thermal shown
- Outer boundary and concentric rings hidden
- Green/red optimum turn-rate guidance shown
- Flight track shown at 35% opacity

Title:
Put it all together

Instructions:
Put all the steps together to find the halfway point and then center at the optimum rate.  The dashed circles are now hidden so rely mostly on the vario sound.  The fuzzy green thermal highlight is still shown.

Goal:
Find and center the thermal, then fly two consecutive orbits staying near optimal turn-rate while staying centered on the thermal

Popup guidance before the 90-degree turn:
[If the user turns before the vario peak]
DON'T TURN
Fly straight until the vario peaks

[Just as or after the vario peaks]
START TURNING NOW
Turn about 90 degrees

Popup guidance after the 90-degree turn:
[Constant vario]
VARIO STEADY
Hold the optimum turn rate

[Decreasing vario]
VARIO DECREASING
tighten your turn to move closer to the center

[Increasing vario]
VARIO INCREASING
widen your turn to move away from the center

Completion feedback:
Great! You found and centered the thermal with minimal visual help.

======================================================================
STAGE 2 — THERMAL SIZES
======================================================================

Stage setup:
- Uses an 80 m thermal
- Visual guidance is progressively removed across three steps
- Green/red optimum turn-rate guidance shown throughout


STEP 1 — SMALL THERMAL WITH VISUAL HELP
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Presentation:
- Fuzzy green thermal, outer boundary, and concentric rings shown

Title:
Understanding Thermal Sizes

Instructions:
The previous thermal we explored required us to fly straight into it for 7 to 8 seconds before we detected the middle using the vario peak. Count how many seconds it takes to find the middle of this thermal, then try to circle it like before.

Goal:
fly straight, count the seconds until the vario peaks, then turn and try to stay in lift for 2 consecutive orbits.

Popup guidance:
[Just as or after the vario peaks]
START TURNING NOW
Turn about 90 degrees

[Constant vario]
VARIO STEADY
Hold a constant turn rate

[Decreasing vario]
VARIO DECREASING
tighten your turn to move closer to the center

[Increasing vario]
VARIO INCREASING
widen your turn to move away from the center

Completion feedback:
Great job coring a small thermal. You probably counted about 4 seconds between entering the thermal and finding the vario peak.


STEP 2 — SMALL THERMAL WITHOUT CIRCLE GUIDANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Presentation:
- Fuzzy green thermal shown
- Outer boundary and concentric rings hidden
- Three-minute time limit

Title:
Step 2: Small thermal without circle guidance

Instructions:
Try the same thermal again, this time without the circle guide rings (the fuzzy green thermal is still shown). Remember to practice counting seconds until the peak to get a sense for how large the thermal is.

Initial goal:
fly straight, count the seconds until the vario peaks, then turn and try to stay in lift for 2 consecutive orbits.

Popup guidance:
Uses the same peak and vario-trend guidance as Stage 2, Step 1.

Completion feedback:
Great job. Without the guide rings, you must rely on the audible vario changes and react quickly and precisely.

Timeout feedback:
Time limit reached. It's much harder to stay in lift when a thermal is small.  You have to react quickly to the vario changes, and even predict the needed turn adjustments a second or two ahead of time.


STEP 3 — SMALL THERMAL BY VARIO ONLY
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Presentation:
- Thermal visualization, outer boundary, and concentric rings hidden
- Three-minute time limit

Title:
Step 3: Small thermal by vario only

Instructions:
Now try the same small thermal with no visual help. In real flight, you only have the vario sound. For practice, keep counting seconds until the peak. But eventually you'll start to get a feel for the size of the thermal without counting.

Initial goal:
fly straight, count the seconds until the vario peaks, then turn and try to stay in lift for 2 consecutive orbits.

Popup guidance:
Uses the same peak and vario-trend guidance as Stage 2, Step 1.

Completion feedback:
Great job! This is a good lesson that a ~4 second thermal might be about the smallest you can stay in. Any smaller and it might be worth passing by until you find a larger thermal.

Timeout feedback:
Time limit reached. As your thermal skills improve, you may be able to stay in lift this small. But this is a good exercise to determine what thermals are worth turning in, and which ones might be worth passing by.
